# Appendices

This directory contains the detailed appendix materials associated with the paper "Automated design for manufacturing and assembly of prefabricated wall panels using BIM and evolutionary algorithms".

The materials are organized as follows:

- **Appendix B – Dynamo programs:** Detailed Dynamo workflows and configurations, including Figs. 19 and 20.
- **Appendix C – Algorithmic details:** Complete pseudocode for Algorithms 1–3.
- **Appendix D – Hyperparameter sensitivity analysis:** Detailed sensitivity analysis results, including Tables 12 and 13.
- **Appendix E – Manual design of the two building cases:** Detailed manual design procedures and calculation results, including Tables 14 and 15.
- **Appendix F – Detailed case study results:** Detailed case study data and BIM visualizations, including Tables 16–19 and Figs. 21–22.

All appendix materials are provided in PDF format, and the original figure, table, and algorithm numbering is preserved to maintain consistency with the manuscript.
